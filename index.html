Narrative-driven video game

Peaceland

Peaceland is a narrative video game about the generation growing up after a war. Players uncover their country's past through memories kept in a museum, and their choices shape its future.

On the Narrative and UX/UI design teams, I built the world and characters, designed branching decisions, and helped design the in-game notebook. I also led playtesting across the Balkans, where players said the game felt made "for them."

Peaceland · Research journal

Opportunity to be an innovative designer

I joined Peaceland to help build a story-first game about the generation growing up after a war, and to research whether it can reduce inherited bias.

A message this important only reaches people if they want to play, so we put just as much care into the game's art, UX/UI and graphic design. That is where my research question came from, and it went on to win a 2026 Schwartz Discover Grant.

I'm Ana Galic, a multidisciplinary designer with a strong foundation in Computer Science and Digital Design.

I create visual identities, graphics for video games, and user-centered websites. Recently that has meant building the world, characters, and in-game notebook for Peaceland, leading the team behind Soulshine, a focus lamp with a companion app, and developing VALS, a multilingual AI voice assistant, from front end to back end.

My research for Peaceland ran in three languages and took me to playtests across the Balkans. It earned a Schwartz Discover Grant from the University of Rochester.
