Multi-sensory focus lamp, companion app & website

Soulshine

linter-berry-84402425.figma.site / aboutVisit live site ↗

Scroll inside the window, or switch pages above

Soulshine is a desk lamp and companion app that gently pulls people out of mindless scrolling. Instead of adding another screen, it uses calm light, sound, scent and vibration to bring your attention back.

I led our team of four as Team Lead and Visual Designer. I ran our workflow, budget and timeline, developed the user personas, and shaped the design vision. I also designed and built the Soulshine website you can explore above, and created its logo and brand.

Designing the website

Built by me

Figma Sites

I designed and built the whole website myself in Figma Sites, as six pages that take visitors from the problem to the product, research, timeline, budget and team.

A choice for the viewer

Light and dark mode

Many people prefer having the option, so every page works in both a light and a dark mode, switched with the sun icon in the menu.

Photoshop, then Figma

Logo and brand

I designed the original Soulshine logo in Adobe Photoshop, then rebuilt it as a 3D lamp in Figma. I chose blue because it is calming and pleasant to the eyes, which suits a product about protecting your attention.

Logo

From a flat sketch to a 3D lamp

I first designed the logo as a flat shape in Adobe Photoshop: a lamp shade carrying the Soulshine wordmark, with warm light falling from underneath. I then rebuilt it as a 3D model in Figma and made three versions of it.

3D logo · Figma · three versions

The problem

Scrolling damages focus, disrupts sleep, and harms mental health. We grounded the project in 11 academic sources on short-form content, attention span, memory and wellbeing, then ran our own survey of students and young professionals.

Survey

71%say scrolling has hurt their ability to focus

Survey

74%have lost track of time while scrolling

Survey

61%want a physical device to remind them to stop

The gap we found

What already exists

Apps and lockboxes

Forest, One Sec, Opal and physical phone lockboxes each tackle part of the problem, either on the screen or by locking the phone away.

What was missing

Physical and digital, together

None of them combine physical and digital signals into one calm experience, even though research shows paired interventions work best. That gap is what Soulshine fills.

How it works

The lamp

A calm cue on your desk

A multi-colored LED light that slowly dims as a session goes on, a scent diffuser, a 360° speaker, a phone stand that keeps your phone charging but out of direct sight, and simple touch buttons for brightness and the timer.

The companion app

Tracking without stress

An iOS and Android app that tracks time on TikTok and Instagram, runs focus sessions with music and blocked apps, and shows a virtual lamp that dims slowly, with no guilt.

From concept to launch

  1. January 2026

    Research and brainstorming

    Literature reviews and first ideas for both the hardware and the software.

  2. February 2026

    Concept and wireframes

    Finalized the idea, gathered input from potential users using iZone resources, planned how the team would work, and wireframed the companion app's UI and core features.

  3. March 2026

    Bringing the parts together

    Used StudioX at the University of Rochester to connect the lamp's light, sound and scent with the app's tracking and reminders.

  4. April 2026

    Securing the investment

    Secured a $100,000 investment for the first 50 lamps, on four conditions: build fast and to a high standard, make it accessible for users with mobility issues, explain the research so anyone can understand it, and account for every dollar.

  5. May 2026

    Prototype, website and final presentation

    Finished and tested a working lamp and app, built and launched the website, and delivered the final presentation with a transparent breakdown of how the $100,000 was spent.

About

Hi, I'm Ana!

I'm a multidisciplinary designer double majoring in Computer Science and Digital Media Studies at the University of Rochester, with a focus in Psychology. I love complex problems, especially ones that call for creative thinking, and I feel most at home where logic, design, and human needs meet.

My work spans UX/UI, graphic design, and human-computer interaction. At Peaceland Research, I took an interactive game from concept to prototype and playtested it across 4 international regions. On the iZone's graphic design team, I create social media graphics and campaigns, including for a TEDx event. I've also designed Soulshine, a digital wellbeing platform, and VALS, an AI voice assistant that turns documents into audio summaries in 6 languages.

I believe technology should make life easier and more accessible, and I'm especially curious about how people think, play, and interact. Most of my inspiration comes from the people and the nature around me. People remind me who I'm designing for. Nature shows me that beauty and purpose can be the same thing, since every curve of a canyon wall and every vein in a leaf is there for a reason.

Feel free to look around my projects, and you can find my resume, LinkedIn, or a direct link to email me right below. I'd love to connect, so reach out!
